Date: 2026-11-24
  Bugfixes:
    - Spreading trees no longer take root on planned builds. Ghosts don't collide with trees, so seeds used to land under them and block the construction robots.

Version: 0.1.4
Date: 2026-11-24
  Features:
    - Tree saplings burn as fuel: 8 MJ, the same as the 4 wood a felled tree gives.
  Changes:
    - The sapling recipe costs 4 wood (was 2), matching its fuel value, so crafting saplings to burn gains nothing.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.3
Date: 2026-11-22
  Features:
    - Trees that lost leaves to pollution regrow them once their chunk's pollution falls below a configurable limit (default 60, never above the level where pollution damages trees). A bare tree regrows fully in 30 minutes by default.
    - New GMO trees technology (200 red + green science): trees regrow leaves at any pollution level. Pollution still strips them, so forests next to the factory keep absorbing pollution instead of wearing out.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.2
Date: 2026-11-15
  Changes:
    - Updated for Factorio 2.1: sapling drop chance uses independent_probability.
    - Sapling recipe no longer sets the removed recipe `category` field.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.1
Date: 2026-09-06
  Features:
    - Forests spread onto natural ground only, so trees ringed by paving stay solitary and decorative.
    - Mining a living tree can drop a tree sapling; the Forestry technology unlocks crafting saplings from wood.
    - Planted saplings grow into the dominant tree species within 16 tiles.
    - A local density cap makes forests creep outward rather than thicken indefinitely.
    - Spreading skips polluted ground by default, and dead or dry trees never propagate.
